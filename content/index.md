An open library of design patterns for building better user-facing applications backed by decentralized architectures. These patterns help developers and designers understand the unique challenges of decentralized systems and provide proven solutions for common problems.

> [!tip] How to use this library
> Browse patterns by topic below — [[identity-agency|Identity & Agency]], [[moderation-curation|Moderation & Curation]], [[sharing-permissions|Sharing & Permissions]], [[sync-status|Sync & Status]] — explore them through the **sidebar**, or follow the links between related patterns. Use the **graph view** to discover connections. Spot a gap or a mistake? Every page has an edit link — this library grows through [[contribute|contributions]] like yours.
